A little about this website

Healthy habits begin with understanding.

Mind & Body is an educational website created to encourage people to take care of their overall well-being. It brings together approachable information about mental wellness, physical activity, nutrition, and healthy daily routines.

Our goal is to make reliable health information easier to understand and turn into practical everyday habits. We believe that caring for your mind is just as important as caring for your body.

Who is this for?
Students, young adults, families, and anyone who wants to learn healthy habits and make informed choices about everyday wellness.
Learn • Practice • Grow

Three foundations of a healthier you.

Explore these general health topics and choose one small action you can practice today.

🧠

1. Mental Wellness

Mental wellness includes how we think, feel, cope with challenges, and connect with other people. It deserves attention just like physical health.

  • Talk with someone you trust about how you feel.
  • Take short breaks when you feel overwhelmed.
  • Try slow breathing, journaling, or a calming activity.
  • Ask for professional support when distress persists.
Remember: Stress is a normal response, but ongoing or overwhelming stress deserves care and support.
🏃

2. Physical Activity

Moving your body can support heart health, strength, mood, and everyday functioning. Choose activities that fit your abilities and circumstances.

  • Walk, dance, cycle, stretch, or play a sport.
  • Break up long periods of sitting when possible.
  • Start gently and increase activity gradually.
  • Make rest and recovery part of your routine.
Helpful guide: WHO recommends adults aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate activity across a week, when able.
🥗

3. Nutrition & Sleep

Food provides energy and nutrients, while sleep helps the body and brain recover. Both are important parts of everyday well-being.

  • Eat a variety of vegetables, fruits, and whole foods.
  • Drink water regularly throughout the day.
  • Keep a consistent sleep and wake schedule.
  • Create a calm, comfortable bedtime routine.
Helpful guide: Most adults aged 18–60 need at least 7 hours of sleep each night.
